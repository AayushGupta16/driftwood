Definition

AI SDR

An AI SDR is software that does the outbound work of a sales development representative: researching prospects, drafting outreach, sending it, and following up on replies. The same category is also sold as an AI sales agent or AI sales rep.

What is an AI SDR?

SDR stands for sales development representative: the person at the top of a sales team whose job is to find prospects and start conversations, which someone else then closes. An AI SDR is software doing that job. Some tools only assist a human with drafting; some run the whole loop on their own; most sit in between.

The category goes by several names. AI sales agent and AI sales rep describe the same tools, with agent usually signaling software that plans and acts in a loop rather than running a fixed sequence. AI BDR meant the same thing and is fading from use. Whatever the label, the products differ on three questions: how much of the job runs automatically, what the message actually carries, and whether a human approves each send before it goes out.

What does an AI SDR actually do?

The job breaks into four parts, and most tools cover some or all of them.

  1. Research: reading what is public about the person and the company, and deciding whether and why to reach out.
  2. Drafting: writing the message for that specific prospect, usually for email or LinkedIn.
  3. Sending: scheduling sends, managing deliverability, and rotating inboxes where the tool uses its own accounts.
  4. Follow-up: bumping non-responders, spotting replies, and handing real conversations to a person.

Where tools differ most is not this list but the output: what the prospect actually receives. That is where the category splits.

What most AI SDR tools get wrong

Most tools in the category compete on volume. The pitch is more sends per dollar: more prospects touched, more sequences running, more inboxes in rotation. Personalization in these tools is copy-deep. A merge tag fills in the first name, a scraped detail supplies the opener, and a language model restates your homepage in a friendly tone. It costs almost nothing to produce, and it reads that way.

Prospects adapted quickly. Cold reply rates were low before this wave, and generic AI-written outreach has pushed them lower; buyers now pattern-match the style and delete it unread. The volume also has costs the dashboard does not show: burned sending domains, mail that lands in spam, and lead lists that stop being usable. More sends is not the same thing as more replies.

What does an AI SDR cost?

As of mid-2026, the market prices in three rough bands. Simple sequencing and outreach automation runs about $50 to $500 a month. Dedicated AI SDR platforms mostly land between $500 and $3,000 a month, priced per seat or per send volume. Enterprise AI SDR agents are custom quoted, usually four figures a month on annual contracts, and many vendors do not publish numbers at all.

AI SDR vs human SDR: what is the difference?

Cost and judgment. A human SDR in the US typically costs $80,000 to $120,000 a year once salary, commission, tools, and management are counted, and brings judgment about who to target and what to say. An AI SDR costs a fraction of that and covers far more prospects, but left alone it produces generic outreach. The setups that work keep both: software does the research, drafting, and follow-up, and a person decides what actually goes out.

Do AI SDRs work?

Volume-first ones mostly do not: they produce more sends, not more replies, and reply rates on generic AI-written outreach are poor. Results change when each message carries something specific the prospect can verify.
