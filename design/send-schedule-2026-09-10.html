Send schedule

Autosana asked to choose when their demos go out. Today the backend has one window for everyone: 9 AM to 6 PM Pacific, seven days a week, no holidays. This draft gives each workspace its own schedule: the days, one daily window, a timezone, and a switch to skip US federal holidays. It applies to email, LinkedIn and X alike, and daily caps still sit on top. Three screens are drawn below. A is the recommended new Settings page. B is the alternative, a single line on Overview. C is the Review queue, which changes either way.
