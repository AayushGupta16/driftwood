Triggers

A new dashboard page at /dashboard/triggers. A trigger reads as one sentence: when something new appears on a site (any URL the customer pastes), then the agent acts on it. The first case is MochaCare watching mycnajobs.com every night for home care agency job postings. Each new posting becomes the agency as a company, the owner or administrator as a lead, a personalized demo of a MochaCare job listing filled with that agency's own posting, and an enrollment in a chosen campaign. Checks run on a schedule; the agent works out how to read each site, so a trigger shows as Building until it has, and Not supported when it cannot. Nothing sends without approval in the Review queue. Five states are drawn below, then the decisions still open.

Triggers

New trigger

Paste a site and say what counts as new. Your agent works out how to check it and does the rest.

When

Something new appears on a site.

Any page that lists the postings. Your agent works out how to read it.

Options All US · Every night, 2 AM PT · 8 employer types skipped

Words a posting should contain. Press Enter or type a comma after each one.

All US

Press Enter after each place, so a city can keep its state.

senior living health system hospital medical center nursing home assisted living memory care hospice

Postings from these employers are skipped. Remove a chip to keep them.

Schedule

Checks run on their own, in Pacific time. Nightly leaves the night for building demos, so the Review queue is ready in the morning.

Then

What your agent does with each new posting.

Queues the campaign's messages for that contact.

Every message waits in your Review queue. Nothing goes out on its own.

Name

Optional. Without one, the trigger is named after the site.

Drawn without asking: Triggers sits after Campaigns in the sidebar; a trigger has no name of its own and is identified by its source and what it watches; the campaign name is written "MochaCare intro for home care agencies" so the copy carries no dashes.

Revised 2026-09-02, per the founder: the trigger is the sentence "When ..., then ..."; any URL is the site, no site is a first-class choice; the customer describes what counts as new in plain words and the agent configures the pull, so the form needs only the site and that line (keywords, locations, skipped employers and the schedule sit under Options with their defaults in view); checks run nightly at 2 AM PT by default (question 4 above is answered), weekly, or every N hours, and "Run now" is the quiet "Check now" link under the facts; the runs log shows pages, seen, new and filtered; a trigger waiting on its pull shows "Building", one on a site we cannot read shows "Not supported" (this replaces the "Account for this site" box that was drawn here); Edit on the detail page opens the same form with the site read-only. A trigger may carry a name of its own after all, typed at the bottom of the form.
