# Visual direction: Customer growth workspace

## Locked direction

- Vibe: minimal + `operator canvas`.
- Palette: preserve `paper`, `surface`, `ink`, `line`, `tide`, `tide-deep`, and `tide-wash` from `landing/src/index.css`.
- Typography: preserve Public Sans for UI and Source Serif 4 for the Driftwood wordmark.
- Spatial language: compact Workbench extended into a persistent left-sidebar app shell; lead lists use a dense data grid, assets use a practical library, metrics use a funnel summary with a people drilldown, and the campaign builder retains its three-pane canvas.
- Design variance: 5/10.
- Visual density: 9/10.
- Effect layer: none. The dotted canvas is functional orientation, not decoration.
- Motion intensity: 1/3.
- Motion personality: Corporate.
- Motion constants: `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`; 200ms / 300ms / 400ms; crisp slide for drawers and menus.
- Smooth scroll: native.
- Illustration style: none; custom geometric outlined SVG icons only.

## Commitment audit

1. Type to vibe: 9/10. Public Sans and the quiet wordmark suit an operator surface.
2. Palette to vibe: 9/10. Tide blue carries action without competing with data.
3. Spatial to vibe: 9/10. The sidebar establishes durable wayfinding while each work surface expresses its job directly.
4. Palette consistency: 10/10. Existing single-accent system is preserved.
5. Typography pair: 9/10. Serif is reserved for brand; sans handles dense UI.
6. Vibe commitment: 9/10. The workspace reads as a precise outbound control surface.

Total: 55/60, pass.
