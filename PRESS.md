### 3. Axios (the AI-citation prize; no named reporter yet)
- VERIFIED: Axios is a top-3 ChatGPT-cited domain in 13 of 17
  industries (Muck Rack, May 2026, 25M+ links) — the only journalism
  outlet at that level. One Axios mention feeds the GEO citation pool
  disproportionately. No named sales-tech reporter survived
  verification — finding the right byline is an open task.
- VERIFIED mechanics: unlinked brand MENTIONS correlate ~3x more
  strongly with AI-answer visibility than backlinks (Ahrefs, 75k
  brands; correlation not causation). "Vanity" coverage without links
  still feeds GEO. Do not chase links at the expense of mentions.

### 4. Journalist-request platforms (background engine, tracked pilot)
- VERIFIED (BuzzStream 2025, cite it directly): **Qwoted** has the
  highest-authority request pool (70.3% of requests from DR 70-100
  outlets); **Source of Sources** (free, Shankman's HARO successor)
  has the highest measured dofollow rate (36.26% vs 21.9% average).
- EVERY specific acceptance-rate number was REFUTED (the ~14% base
  rate, Featured 18-20%, Qwoted ~6%). Real hit rates are unknown; run
  a 60-90 day in-house pilot: answer SOS (free) + Qwoted requests in
  the AI/sales/founder lanes, log platform/DR/link-type per response,
  and let our own base rate emerge.
