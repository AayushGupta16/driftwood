What the GEO probe actually does (the prompt, plainly)

For each of the 100 fixed queries, a subagent runs ONE web search with the query text, verbatim, then answers three questions about the results page it got back:

  1. Does driftwood.sh appear anywhere in the results? (yes + position / no) - with explicit instructions that driftwood.ai, driftwood-ai.com, and literal wood do NOT count.
  2. Which of the 16 watch-list competitors appear? (Instantly, Artisan, 11x, Apollo, Clay, Autobound, ...)
  3. What are the top 3 result domains?

That is the whole prompt - no opinion, no scoring by the model, just presence extraction. The scoring (tier hit rates, leaderboards) happens in code afterward. The premise: when a founder asks ChatGPT/Claude to research outreach, the assistant runs searches shaped like these queries and reads what comes back - so "does driftwood appear in these results" approximates "will an AI assistant ever meet driftwood". It measures the search layer, not final AI answers; answer-checking (asking real engines and grading their replies) is the planned layer 2 once the backend runner exists.
