Founder-led outbound, under 1% replies

Autosana builds AI agents for mobile QA. Like most early-stage founders, Yuvan Sundrani ran cold outbound himself. He found the leads, wrote the messages, and sent them from his own accounts.

It mostly did not work. His self-reported reply rate was under 1%. Prospects who fit Autosana perfectly never answered at all.

What driftwood did

driftwood is an AI sales agent for cold outbound. For each prospect on Yuvan's existing list, the agent researched the company, then built a working demo of Autosana for their business: Autosana's agent running against the prospect's own product.

Every message went through human review before it sent. Nothing left the queue without approval. And every message went out from Yuvan's own account, LinkedIn or email, so replies landed where he already works.

The review queue. A human approves every message before it sends.
