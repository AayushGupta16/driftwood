Subject: Founder story pitch from a UMD alum: driftwood

Hi,

I am Aayush Gupta, UMD [class year], [degree]. I have been reading the
Founder Stories on the Innovation Gateway site and would like to pitch
mine.

After Maryland I was the founding engineer at Autosana (YC S25), an AI
startup that tests mobile apps. This year I left to start driftwood
(driftwood.sh), an AI sales agent for cold outreach. Instead of
sending templated messages, it researches each prospect and builds a
working demo of the sender's product for that prospect's business,
then a human approves every message before it sends.

The part that makes it a story: my old company became my first
customer. Autosana ran driftwood on its own outreach, and replies went
from under 1% to over 14% in week one at Autosana (YC S25), including
founders who had ignored more than four months of prior outreach. I
should be clear about the relationship: I was Autosana's founding
engineer before they became driftwood's first customer, and I disclose
that everywhere the number appears. The full methodology is public at
driftwood.sh/cold-outbound-benchmarks and the customer story is at
driftwood.sh/customers/autosana.

driftwood is part of a16z's speedrun program, cohort A001.

Happy to do an interview and to send a headshot, screenshots, or
anything else useful for the piece.

Aayush
aayush@driftwood.sh
